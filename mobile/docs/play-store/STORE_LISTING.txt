Full description (4000 max, paste into Play Console > Main store listing):
Givy is the easy way to share a gift wishlist for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, holidays, and graduations.

Make one list, add the gifts you actually want, and share a single link. Friends open it in any browser, claim a gift, and everyone else sees it's taken. You never see who claimed what, so the surprise stays a surprise.

WHY PEOPLE USE GIVY
• No more duplicate gifts. Claimed gifts lock for everyone else.
• Claims stay secret. You see what's taken, never who took it.
• Works with any shop. Paste a product link and the photo fills in.
• Group gifts. Friends can chip in together toward the big one.
• Countdown to the day, so friends know how much time is left.
• Your ship-to address is shown only to the person who claims a gift.
• Sign in with email, Google, or Facebook.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Create a list for your occasion
2. Add gifts from any shop
3. Share one link with family and friends
4. They claim gifts privately

Givy is free for personal lists. Purchases happen on the retailer's own website. Givy keeps the list in sync, not the checkout.

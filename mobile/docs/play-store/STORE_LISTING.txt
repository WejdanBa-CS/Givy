Givy

Short description (80 max):
Gift wishlists without duplicates. Share one link; friends claim privately.

Full description (paste into Play Console → Main store listing):
Givy helps you share gift wishlists for birthdays, holidays, weddings, and more — without the awkward duplicate presents.

Create a list, add gift ideas with links and prices, then share one private link with friends and family. When someone claims a gift, you see that it’s taken — but not who claimed it — so surprises stay intact.

WHY GIVY
• One shared list for every occasion
• Anonymous claims — no more “who bought what” spoilers
• Optional ship-to address revealed only to the person who claims a gift
• Optional tip link for supporters (PayPal / similar — handled by them, not Givy)
• Sign in with email, Google, or Facebook

HOW IT WORKS
1. Create your Givy list
2. Add gifts you actually want
3. Share one link
4. Friends claim gifts privately

Purchases happen on retailer websites — Givy coordinates the list, not the checkout.
